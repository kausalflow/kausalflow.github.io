Featured project

Homeostat

Plant data that knows why it moved.

Homeostat generates process-industry time series that behave like a real plant: closed control loops, drifting instruments, hidden disturbances and lab results that arrive hours late. Every value comes with the truth behind it.

  • Live plants. Simulated plants that run day and night, with sensor data every ten minutes and lab results released weekly.
  • A weekly benchmark. Every Friday, predict the past week's lab results from its sensor data, on your own or with an AI coding agent.
  • Open source. A Python package (pip install homeostat) under the Apache-2.0 licence, with full documentation.
