About KausalFlow

Less repetitive work, more science.

We love scientific research, and we see its everyday problems too: data scattered across folders, analyses that are hard to reproduce, more papers than anyone can read. KausalFlow builds open tools that take this work off researchers’ hands, so that they can spend their time on discovery.

What we do

Open tools, from data to discovery

  • Data with ground truthTo judge a method, you need to know what really happened. Homeostat generates process data with every cause recorded, and runs live plants with a weekly benchmark.
  • Automated research workflowsWe help institutes and researchers manage their documents and data with automated workflows: tools that find and summarise papers, connect references and keep datasets in order.
  • Open by defaultWe open source our code and documents, and we build communities around them. We believe openness moves us forward.
